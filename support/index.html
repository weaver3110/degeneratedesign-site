Common questions

Signing in

Oldschool MTG signs you in with a one-time code emailed to your address — there is no password. Enter your email, then the six-digit code we send. If the code doesn't arrive, check spam and try again, or email us.

Subscriptions and billing

The AI Judge offers Free (5 questions/week), Basic ($49.95/year, 25/week), and Pro ($99.95/year, 50/week plus voice and saved history). Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel anytime in your Apple ID subscription settings, or from the app at Profile → Account. Card data, rules, and the life counter are always free.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data in the app at Profile → Account → Delete account, or by emailing support@degeneratedesign.com. Deleting your account is separate from canceling a subscription.

Reporting a wrong ruling or a bug

If the Judge gives an answer that looks wrong, tap Report issue beneath the answer, or email us with the question and what you expected. Bug reports are welcome the same way.

Privacy and data requests

To access, correct, or delete your personal information, email support@degeneratedesign.com. See our Privacy Policy and Terms of Service for details.
